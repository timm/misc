What survived the audit

Two use-case rows stay empty outside the vision column: UC2 (translate a package between venue standards) and UC7 (critique against domain exemplars). UC1 has a single partial (OpenART, abstract only). Those three are the defensible gap.

What did not survive

I had UC5 (what the paper lacks) as empty. Audit found seven partials: Artisan's 20 paper-artifact inconsistencies, ReproRepo's paper/repo mismatch category, Heye's pitfall detector, PaperCoder's "anything unclear" plan node, PaperBench addendums, Argonne's hallucinated-number check, OpenART's inconsistent-component flag. UC3 also rose from three partials to seven. Nobody covers either fully.

Clusters, re-run

Elements: the vision's nearest neighbours are OpenART and Paper2Code at 42% match, then Amougou Mbida and CORE/PaperBench at 40%. Nothing is close. In the tree it joins OpenART, ArtifactCopilot and Amougou Mbida first; the execution cluster (Heye, Repo2Run, Paper2Code, Artisan, CORE/PaperBench) sits at the far end. Constructs: the four empty-or-near-empty use cases still merge at 100%; UC3, UC5 and UC6 reflect together with "starts from the artifact", meaning the tools that do claim links, gap finding and generation are the paper-first ones.

Softer claim than before

Earlier version said prior work sits in the opposite corner. Audited grid says: prior work has partial coverage of most Triple-A use cases as by-products, and the vision's distinct ground is venue-standard translation, exemplar critique, and an author-facing appendix as the deliverable. Bipolar ratings remain judgments, with per-cell confidence in the tooltip.

Ratings: first pass by one reader, then every cell re-rated by an independent read of the PDF with a verbatim quote required (audit JSON in artifacts/appendix/related/audit/).
