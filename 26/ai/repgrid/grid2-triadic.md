## Findings

1. **The two grids disagree about NCSU, hard.** Grid 1 (Provost's published criteria): NCSU ranks #1 of 14. Grid 2 (Tim's elicited criteria): NCSU ties last with UC Davis and Rutgers. Same department, same evidence.
2. **The divergence is entirely political, not curricular.** Grid 2's NCSU penalty comes from G5 turf-lock (5 — worst in set), G1 brand (4), G6 no institutional bet (3). None of these are things taught in a classroom. On classroom substance (grid 1), NCSU already leads.
3. **Evidence contradicts one elicited belief**: T2 grouped UC Davis with Purdue as "shipped AI degrees" — but Davis has shipped nothing (AI major/minor still at student-survey stage; grid-1 C8=4, worst in set). The mental model of Davis is stale; Purdue stands alone as the shipping benchmark.
4. **Where Tim's and the Provost's lenses agree**: credential shipping matters (G2 ↔ C8) and cross-campus reach matters (G3 ↔ C7). These two constructs are the safe common ground for the white paper.
5. **Implication for planv0**: the whole gap between "NCSU as Provost's ideal" and "NCSU as Tim experiences it" is turf + brand + institutional will — precisely planv0 items 1 (quit ownership war), 4 (borrow Provost's frame), 6 (grab fellowship legitimacy). The rep grids are quantitative confirmation of the strategy.
