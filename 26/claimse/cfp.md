Artifact evaluation asks whether a paper's files exist and run. It does not ask whether they support what the paper claims. The ACE framework ([URL]) asks that question instead: **does the supplied evidence support each core scientific claim?**

We invite papers that make this question easy to answer.

## What to include

For each **core scientific claim** (one that a main result or contribution depends on), state:

1. **The claim:** its exact wording and where it appears in the paper.
2. **Its constructs:** what the claim is about, and what those terms mean in the study.
3. **Operationalization:** the indicators that represent each construct, and how their values are obtained from the data.
4. **Analysis and results:** how the results were produced, with locations a reader can inspect (sections, tables, files, code).
5. **Reasoning:** why the authors think the indicators and results support the claim.

Short tables in the artifact deposit are enough. They do not count toward page limits.

## Topics

- Research papers whose core claims come with this mapping.
- Mappings of already-published papers, including what is missing or conflicting.
- Tools and agents that help write or check such mappings.
- Critiques: where claim–evidence mapping fails or costs too much.

ACE was developed for quantitative empirical work. Qualitative, formal and other papers are welcome; say how you adapted the mapping.

## How submissions are assessed

- **No rerunning required.** Assessment is by static inspection of the paper and its supplied materials. Execution is an optional follow-up, never a requirement.
- **Claim by claim.** Reviewers report findings for each core claim. There is no overall grade for the paper.
- **Gaps are allowed.** A claim whose evidence is missing or inaccessible stays in the mapping, marked as such. An honest gap beats a hidden one.

## Submit

The paper, its claim–evidence mapping, and an archived artifact with a persistent identifier where one exists. Mappings drafted with an LLM are allowed; say so, and check them yourself.
